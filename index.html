How calls are counted

A call is one 3-second stretch of audio from a rooftop camera microphone that BirdNET scores at or above the confidence you pick below (50% to start), with optional filters that hold back one-off, rare or out-of-hours calls, filtered to species plausible for the region and week. Rates divide calls by the clips recorded, so hours with more recording don't look busier.

Does the weather change how much birds call?

Each bar compares a kind of weather with a typical day at the same hour, so the morning chorus isn't mistaken for a weather effect. A handful of days is only a few weather systems, so read these as hints. Weather comes hourly from the Sea-Tac airport station.

Every species

Photos from Wikipedia. Hear opens recordings of the species on xeno-canto to compare with what the microphone caught. Confident calls meet the minimum confidence set at the top. Possible calls scored below that line, down to 25%. Held back calls cleared the line but a filter hid them. The badge ignores the sliders: Solid means 75% or higher on two or more days, Plausible means 50% or higher on two or more days, Unverified is everything else. Several days of agreement is evidence, not proof, since a recurring non-bird sound can repeat too.
